I'm an MSc student in mathematics at [Université de Sherbrooke](https://www.usherbrooke.ca/) (Québec, Canada) under the supervision of [Emily Cliff](https://www.usherbrooke.ca/mathematiques/nous-joindre/personnel/corps-professoral/professeurs/emily-cliff) and [Thomas Brüstle](https://www.usherbrooke.ca/mathematiques/nous-joindre/personnel/corps-professoral/professeurs/thomas-brustle). My research interests lie within category theory, algebraic topology, representation theory and topological data analysis.

I obtained my BSc in mathematics from [Yachay Tech University](https://yachaytech.edu.ec/) (Ecuador) at the [School of Mathematics and Computer Science](https://yachaytech.edu.ec/escuela-de-ciencias-matematicas-y-computacionales/) in April 2025.
